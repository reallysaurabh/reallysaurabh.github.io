# About Me

I am an AI Leader, Technical Architect, and Published Researcher with a focus on bridging the gap between cutting-edge AI research and enterprise-scale production. Across startups and Fortune 500 organizations, I have architected and deployed high-impact AI systems—ranging from enterprise LLM gateways and autonomous agent systems to production machine learning models and privacy-preserving algorithms.

### What I Do

- **Enterprise AI & GenAI Platforms**: Architecting scalable, secure AI platforms and LLM gateways (such as MIDAS at Maersk Global) that enable thousands of engineering teams to build governed, observable generative AI applications.
- **AI Solution Architecture & Agentic Systems**: Building production-grade agentic workflows, multi-modal applications, and RAG architectures (including work for Google Extended Workforce on India Data Commons, and the Seba Technology Disruption Framework digital twin for Autonomy Capital).
- **Applied AI Research & Benchmarking**: Leading AI benchmarking and cognitive modeling initiatives (such as ASTOUND), with research published in premier peer-reviewed venues including *Nature Human Behaviour*.
- **Machine Learning & MLOps**: Designing high-precision ML models for enterprise compliance (HS code classification at Pitney Bowes) and optimizing inference latency, throughput, and costs across distributed microservices.

### Research Interests

- Autonomous Agentic Systems & Model Context Protocol (MCP)
- Theory of Mind & Cognitive Benchmarks for AI
- Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning & Differential Privacy
- Large-Scale Search Relevance & Multilingual NLP
